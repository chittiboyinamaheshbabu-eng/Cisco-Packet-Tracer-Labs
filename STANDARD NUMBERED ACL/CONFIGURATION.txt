CONDITIONS
--> IN STANDARD ACL WE NEED IMPLEMENT ACLS AT DESTINATION ROUTER 
1) HOST 192.168.1.2 NOT TO ACCESS NETWORK 192.168.3.0
2) NETWORK 192.168.2.0 NOT TO ACCESS NETWORK 192.168.3.0

#ROUTER BANGLORE

BANGLORE(config)#ACcess-list 10 DENY HOST 192.168.1.2 
BANGLORE(config)#ACcess-list 10 DENY 192.168.2.0 0.0.0.255 
BANGLORE(config)#ACcess-list 10 PERMIT ANY 
BANGLORE(config)#INTERFACE G0/0
BANGLORE(config-if)#IP ACcess-group 10 OUT
